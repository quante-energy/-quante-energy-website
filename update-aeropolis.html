"Aeropolis: 149 kW and Climbing"

Aeropolis sits on Porwal Road in Lohegaon, Pune. At 149 kW DC, it's one of the bigger systems Quante Renewables has put up, and the numbers back that up.

The setup
The site runs on a 149 kW DC rooftop array with full monitoring: irradiance, generated power, predicted power, and current power draw, all live on one dashboard. The owner doesn't have to wait for a monthly report to know how the system is doing. They can pull it up right now.

The numbers
Since commissioning, Aeropolis has put out 129.08 MWh. This year alone it's generated 143,678 kWh, running at an average CUF of 28.29%, which is a strong number for a rooftop system.
June was the standout month: over 62,000 kWh in a single month, more than double any other month this year. April came in second at just over 32,000 kWh, May close behind at around 24,000 kWh. Even the slower months, July and August, still cleared 7,000 to 11,000 kWh each.
Total CO2 saved to date: 129.31 tonnes.

Why it works
Big systems only pay off if they actually perform, and Aeropolis has. 149 kW of capacity turned into consistent generation month after month, with June proving what the site can do when conditions line up. That's the kind of return Quanta Renewables designs for on every project, not just the big ones.
